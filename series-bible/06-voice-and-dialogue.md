# Little Wings — Voice & Dialogue Bible

## Language
Default spoken language: Urdu / Urdu-Hinglish.

## Dialogue Style
Dialogue should be:
- short
- natural
- age-appropriate
- conversational
- emotionally clear
- easy to lip-sync

Children should sound like children, not lecturers.

## Speaker Assignment
Every dialogue line must have an explicit speaker ID.

Example:
CHAR_MOTHER_001: "بیٹا، بس تھوڑا سا چکھ کر دیکھو۔"

## Lip-Sync Rule
Only the currently speaking character may move their lips.

When one character speaks:
- that character's mouth moves
- all silent characters keep their mouths closed
- silent characters may react with eyes, head, hands, or body

Never animate the wrong character's mouth.

## Voice Continuity
A recurring character should use the same approved voice identity whenever possible.

## Timing
Prefer one clear speaker per shot. Split dialogue into separate clips when multiple speakers make lip-sync unreliable.

## Automation Output
Every generated scene must include:
- speaker ID
- exact dialogue
- approximate speaking interval
- silent-character instruction
