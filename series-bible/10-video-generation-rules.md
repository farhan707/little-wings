# Little Wings — Video Generation Rules

## Primary Goal
Generate short, coherent clips while preserving canon.

## Prompt Language
Visual prompts should normally be written in English for stronger model reliability.

Dialogue remains in the required spoken language.

## Clip Design
Each clip should normally be 5–10 seconds and focus on one clear action.

## Motion
Prefer:
- natural walking
- natural hand movement
- subtle head movement
- believable reactions
- gentle camera movement

Avoid excessive motion and simultaneous complex actions.

## Strict Lip-Sync
Use this instruction whenever dialogue is present:

**IMPORTANT STRICT LIP-SYNC: Only the character currently speaking may move their lips and mouth. Silent characters must keep their mouths closed. Never animate the wrong character speaking. Match each dialogue line to the exact character shown speaking.**

## Character Continuity
The generation prompt must preserve approved face, hair, clothes, accessories, proportions, and age appearance.

## Environment Continuity
Use the approved environment reference and preserve major layout, furniture, props, lighting, and camera relationship.

## Generation Failure
If identity, lip-sync, anatomy, environment, or continuity fails, mark the clip for regeneration rather than accepting a visibly inconsistent result.
