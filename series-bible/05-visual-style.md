# Little Wings — Visual Style Bible

## Target Look
Premium 3D children's animation with a polished cinematic finish.

## Required Characteristics
- expressive but natural faces
- appealing child proportions
- soft cinematic lighting
- warm color atmosphere
- clean, detailed environments
- believable materials
- natural body movement
- natural facial expressions
- cinematic composition

## Camera
Prefer stable medium and wide compositions. Use subtle camera movement only when it improves the scene.

Avoid unnecessary rapid zooms, excessive camera shake, or complicated camera choreography.

## Continuity
Character identity and environment continuity take priority over visual novelty.

## Negative Style Rules
Avoid:
- photorealistic humans
- horror aesthetics
- distorted anatomy
- exaggerated facial deformation
- random costume changes
- inconsistent age appearance
- unrelated background redesigns
- excessive motion blur

## Prompt Principle
Describe the intended visual result clearly. Do not rely on vague phrases such as "make it like before" when an exact canon rule can be stated.
