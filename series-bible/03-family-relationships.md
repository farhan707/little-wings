# Little Wings — Family Relationships

## Family Structure
- Older sister: CHAR_GIRL_001
- Younger brother: CHAR_BOY_001
- Mother: CHAR_MOTHER_001
- Father: CHAR_FATHER_001

## Sibling Dynamic
The siblings love each other. They may:
- play together
- tease harmlessly
- disagree about small things
- compete playfully
- help each other
- reconcile naturally

Conflict must remain age-appropriate and non-harmful.

## Parent Dynamic
Parents are caring, patient, protective, supportive, and emotionally available.

Parents guide children through experiences rather than constantly lecturing them.

## Family Tone
Interactions should feel like a real family: warmth, humor, small reactions, affection, and natural interruptions.

## Safety
No unnecessary abuse, cruelty, humiliation, disturbing situations, or dangerous behavior.

## Automation Rule
Relationship behavior must remain consistent even when the story changes location or subject.
