# Little Wings — Series Identity

## Series
**Name:** Little Wings  
**Type:** 3D animated family cartoon series  
**Audience:** Children and families

## Core Theme
Everyday adventures, discoveries, funny moments, harmless disagreements, learning experiences, and warm family interactions.

## Tone
Warm, cute, funny, playful, innocent, family-friendly, emotionally positive, occasionally mischievous, lighthearted.

## Story Philosophy
Stories should feel natural rather than like lessons disguised as stories. A lesson may emerge from a relatable situation, discovery, family interaction, harmless problem, or emotional moment.

## Cultural Setting
Warm South Asian / Pakistani family environment. Food, clothing, language, home life, and family behavior should feel culturally authentic and broadly relatable.

## Language
- Spoken dialogue: Urdu / Urdu-Hinglish by default.
- Visual-generation prompts: English.
- Technical instructions: English.

## Visual Identity
Premium 3D children's animation:
- expressive faces
- child-friendly proportions
- soft cinematic lighting
- clean detailed environments
- warm family atmosphere
- natural movement
- cinematic composition

## Non-Negotiable
Consistency is more important than unnecessary creativity.
