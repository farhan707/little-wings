# Little Wings — World & Environment Bible

## World
A warm, recognizable South Asian/Pakistani family world.

## Environment Principles
Environments must support character continuity and should not change arbitrarily between clips.

For recurring locations, lock:
- room layout
- furniture placement
- wall colors/materials
- doors/windows
- major props
- lighting direction
- time-of-day appearance
- table/chair design
- recurring food and household objects

## Core Locations
Potential recurring locations include:
- kitchen
- dining room
- bedroom
- living room
- garden/outdoor area
- school

A location becomes canon only when explicitly approved.

## Environment Continuity
When a story remains in the same location:
- do not redesign the room between clips
- do not move major furniture without story justification
- do not randomly change lighting or time of day
- reuse approved environment references

## Master Reference Strategy
Prefer a small number of strong master references over generating a new environment for every scene.

## Automation Rule
Before generating a scene, automation must identify the location and select an existing master environment reference when one exists.
