## Canon Environment

The approved dining master is the primary visual identity source for the Little Wings family breakfast/dining environment.

### Fixed Layout
- Cozy modern South Asian family kitchen-dining space
- Wooden rectangular dining table
- Matching wooden dining chairs
- Blue patterned table runner
- Warm wood flooring
- Kitchen visible in the background
- Refrigerator and open shelving
- Indoor plants
- Framed family-friendly artwork
- Large window with a bright garden view
- Warm pendant dining lights

### Canon Atmosphere
- Bright, warm, welcoming family-home feeling
- Default time: morning breakfast
- Soft natural daylight combined with warm indoor lighting
- Premium 3D children's animation style

### Canon Props
- Plates and bowls
- Breakfast food and serving dishes
- Egg paratha
- Yogurt
- Chutney
- Juice
- Fruit bowl
- Tea/coffee cups

### Continuity Rules
Do not arbitrarily change the table, chair arrangement, window placement, kitchen layout, major furniture, lighting design, wall décor, or primary color palette between scenes.

New props may be introduced when required by a story, but permanent environment changes require an explicit canon update.
