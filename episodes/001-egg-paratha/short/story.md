## Story
During breakfast, the boy happily enjoys his egg paratha while his sister refuses to eat hers. Their mother gently explains that eggs provide protein to help the body grow strong and that paratha provides energy for play. She encourages the girl to take a small bite. The girl tries it, discovers that she likes it, and the family ends the breakfast moment with warmth and happiness.
