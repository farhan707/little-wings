# Episode 001 — Production Plan

## Global Production Rules

- Target clip duration: 5–10 seconds.
- Prefer one primary speaker per dialogue clip.
- One primary action per clip.
- Maintain ENV_DINING_001 continuity.
- Use approved character references only.
- For scenes 1–9, do NOT use pending REF_MASTER_MOTHER_CHILDREN_DINING_001; use approved individual character references plus ENV_DINING_001.
- Scene 10 may use REF_MASTER_FAMILY_DINING_001.
- Strict lip-sync applies to every dialogue clip.

## Global Video Prompt Lock

Every generated video prompt must preserve:
- premium 3D Little Wings cartoon style
- exact canonical character face, hair, clothing, proportions and accessories
- exact ENV_DINING_001 layout
- warm morning breakfast lighting
- natural child/family movement
- stable cinematic camera
- no character morphing
- no environment redesign

IMPORTANT STRICT LIP-SYNC:
Only the character currently speaking may move their lips and mouth. All silent characters must keep their mouths closed. Never animate the lips of the wrong character. Match each dialogue line to the exact character shown speaking.
